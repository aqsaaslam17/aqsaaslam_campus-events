Top Tourist Destinations

Hunza Valley

Hunza is famous for its breathtaking mountain views, peaceful environment, and rich culture. It is one of the most popular tourist destinations in Pakistan.

Skardu

Skardu offers stunning lakes, cold deserts, and access to some of the highest mountains in the world including K2.

Swat Valley

Known as the "Switzerland of Pakistan", Swat is full of green valleys, rivers, and scenic beauty.

Lahore

Lahore is rich in history and culture, featuring places like Lahore Fort, Badshahi Mosque, and vibrant food streets.

Murree

A popular hill station near Islamabad, known for cool weather, beautiful hills, and tourist attractions.
